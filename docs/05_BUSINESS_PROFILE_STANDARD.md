# Business Profile Standard

## Purpose

This document defines the standard for every business profile on DinLinks.

Every company profile must follow these principles.

---

# Mission

A visitor should be able to decide whether to contact a business within a few minutes.

The profile should answer the most important customer questions without needing to visit another website.

---

# Decision Goal

The purpose of every business profile is not simply to display information.

Its purpose is to help customers decide whether this is the right business for them.

---

# Information Priority

Information must always appear in this order.

1. Business Identity
2. Trust & Verification
3. Business Overview
4. Locations
5. Services
6. Contact
7. Reviews
8. Additional Information

---

# Required Information

Every profile should contain:

- Business name
- Category
- Logo
- Cover image
- Short description
- Address
- Opening hours
- Phone number
- Email
- Website (optional if unavailable)

---

# Optional Information

Businesses may also include:

- Social media links
- Frequently asked questions
- Certifications
- Awards
- Payment methods
- Accessibility information
- Languages spoken

Optional information should enrich the profile without distracting from the core information.

---

# Trust Information

Show trust before marketing.

Examples:

- Verified business
- Organization number
- Established year
- Number of employees
- Business size
- Areas served

Trust should never feel like advertising.

---

# Business Story

Every business should have a story.

The story explains:

- who they are
- what they do
- why they started
- what makes them different

Avoid marketing language.

Write naturally.

---

# Business Overview

Every profile should clearly answer:

- What does this business do?
- Who is it for?
- Where does it operate?
- Why should someone choose it?

Users should understand the business within a few seconds.

---

# Services

Services should be easy to scan.

Each service may contain:

- Name
- Description
- Starting price (optional)

Avoid long paragraphs.

---

# Media

High-quality images only.

Recommended:

- Cover image
- Logo
- Gallery
- Interior
- Exterior
- Team
- Products or work examples

Avoid low-quality or blurry photos.

---

# Media Quality

Images should represent the real business.

Avoid stock photos whenever possible.

Authentic photography builds trust.

---

# Location

Location must always include:

- Address
- Map
- Directions
- Service area

Multiple locations should be supported.

---

# Contact

Customers should immediately find:

- Phone
- Email
- Website
- Booking link (if available)

Never hide contact information.

---

# Reviews

Reviews should help users make decisions.

Quality is more important than quantity.

---

# Profile Completeness

Profiles should encourage businesses to complete all important information.

A complete profile builds more trust than an incomplete one.

Always prioritize completeness over decoration.

---

# Profile Quality

Every profile should feel:

- Complete
- Reliable
- Honest
- Professional

Never feel:

- Empty
- Salesy
- Spammy
- Outdated

---

# Key Principle

Every profile should help visitors answer one question:

"Is this the right business for me?"

# SEO Standard

## Purpose

SEO is how people discover businesses on DinLinks.

Every business profile should be easy to find through search engines.

---

# Mission

Help the right users find the right businesses through organic search.

SEO should improve discoverability without reducing user experience.

---

# SEO Philosophy

Write for people first.

Optimize for search engines second.

A great user experience creates better SEO.

---

# Search Intent

Every page should satisfy a clear search intent.

Examples:

- Business name
- Business category
- Service
- City
- Local searches

Every page should answer the user's question.

---

# Business Pages

Every business page should have:

- Unique title
- Unique meta description
- Clean URL
- Structured headings
- High-quality content

Never create duplicate pages.

---

# Content Quality

Content should be:

- Helpful
- Accurate
- Original
- Up to date

Avoid thin content.

Avoid duplicated content.

---

# Local SEO

Every business should clearly display:

- Business name
- Address
- City
- Opening hours
- Contact information

Location information should always be accurate.

---

# Structured Data

Whenever possible, use structured data.

Examples:

- Local Business
- Organization
- Reviews
- Opening Hours
- Address

Structured data should improve search understanding, not manipulate rankings.

---

# Internal Linking

Pages should naturally link to:

- Categories
- Cities
- Related businesses
- Relevant services

Internal links should help users discover more businesses.

---

# Performance

Fast pages rank better.

SEO and performance should always work together.

Never sacrifice speed for unnecessary design elements.

---

# Images

Images should:

- Have descriptive filenames
- Include alt text
- Be optimized
- Load efficiently

Every image should provide value.

---

# Trust Signals

Search engines should clearly understand that businesses are trustworthy.

Examples:

- Verified business
- Organization number
- Reviews
- Complete profiles
- Accurate information

---

# SEO Ethics

Never manipulate rankings.

Avoid:

- Keyword stuffing
- Hidden text
- Duplicate pages
- Spam content
- Misleading metadata

Long-term trust is more valuable than short-term rankings.

---

# Measurement

Success should be measured by:

- Organic traffic
- Search visibility
- Click-through rate
- Business profile visits
- User engagement

Never optimize only for rankings.

---

# Key Principle

The best SEO comes from building the best business directory.

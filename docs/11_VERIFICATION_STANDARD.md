# Verification Standard

## Purpose

Verification is one of the core trust mechanisms in DinLinks.

This document defines what "Verified" means and how it should be used throughout the platform.

---

# Verification Principle

Verification exists to increase trust.

It must never be used as a marketing tool or as a promise of business quality.

---

# Definition

A verified business is a business whose core legal information has been verified against an official business register or another approved verification source defined by DinLinks.

Verification confirms the identity of the business.

It does not guarantee the quality of its products or services.

---

# Objectives

Verification exists to:

- Increase user trust.
- Improve information accuracy.
- Help users identify legitimate businesses.
- Encourage businesses to maintain accurate information.

---

# What Verification Confirms

Verification confirms that:

- The business legally exists.
- The business information matches official records.
- The business identity has been validated.

---

# What Verification Does NOT Confirm

Verification does NOT guarantee:

- Service quality
- Customer satisfaction
- Professional competence
- Financial stability
- Future business performance

Verification should never be interpreted as a recommendation or endorsement by DinLinks.

---

# Responsibility

Verification confirms the identity of the business at the time of verification.

Businesses remain responsible for keeping their information accurate and up to date.

DinLinks may periodically revalidate verification status to maintain trust.

---

# Verification Requirements

To become verified, a business must satisfy the verification requirements defined by DinLinks.

Verification methods may evolve over time, but every method must prioritize:

- Trust
- Accuracy
- Transparency
- Consistency

---

# Loss of Verification

Verification may be removed if:

- Official information can no longer be verified.
- The business no longer meets verification requirements.
- Fraud or misuse is confirmed.
- The business intentionally provides false information.

Verification removal should follow a documented review process.

---

# Search

Verification is a trust signal.

It may contribute to search ranking.

It must never override relevance.

The most relevant business should always rank first.

---

# User Interface

The verification badge should:

- Be easy to recognize.
- Be visually consistent.
- Never dominate the interface.
- Always represent the same meaning throughout DinLinks.

---

# Transparency

Users should always understand what the verification badge represents.

DinLinks should clearly explain what verification means.

The full verification process does not need to be publicly disclosed.

---

# Future Evolution

Verification methods may change as DinLinks expands into new countries and regions.

The meaning of verification must remain consistent even if the verification process evolves.

---

# Key Principle

Verification builds trust through transparency and accuracy, not through exclusivity.

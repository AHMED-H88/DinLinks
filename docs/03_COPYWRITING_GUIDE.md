# DinLinks Copywriting Guide

## Purpose

Every word in DinLinks should help users make better decisions.

Writing is part of the product.

---

# Writing Goals

Every piece of text should achieve at least one of these goals:

- Inform
- Guide
- Build trust
- Encourage action

Never write text just to fill space.

---

# Writing Philosophy

Write to inform.

Write to build trust.

Write to help.

Never write to impress.

---

# Tone of Voice

DinLinks is:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Honest
- Calm
- Helpful
- Confident

DinLinks is NOT:

- Salesy
- Funny
- Corporate
- Complicated
- Loud
- Clickbait

---

# Writing Style

Write in a calm, direct, and professional way.

Prefer short sentences.

One sentence should communicate one idea.

Avoid decorative punctuation.

Do not use:

- —
- …
- Decorative quotation marks
- Unnecessary parentheses

Prefer simple punctuation:

- .
- ,
- :

---

# Word Consistency

Prefer the simplest commonly accepted spelling.

Examples:

Use:
- Kafe

Instead of:
- Café

Use consistent terminology throughout the entire product.

Do not mix multiple spellings of the same word.

If two words have the same meaning, always use one standardized version across the entire platform.

---

# Abbreviations

Avoid abbreviations unless they are widely understood.

Prefer full words whenever possible.

Good:

Contact information

Bad:

Contact info

Avoid unnecessary abbreviations across all supported languages.

---

# Writing Rules

Always:

- Use short sentences.
- Be specific.
- Use plain language.
- Explain clearly.
- Remove unnecessary words.

Avoid:

- Buzzwords
- Marketing clichés
- Fake urgency
- Exclamation marks unless truly needed
- Technical jargon for customers

---

# Headlines

Headlines should:

- Explain the value.
- Be easy to understand.
- Avoid hype.

Good:

Find trusted businesses.

Bad:

The future of business discovery starts here!

---

# Buttons

Buttons should describe actions.

Good:

Search

Book

Call

Visit Website

Add your business

Avoid:

Click Here

Submit

Continue

---

# Microcopy

Small pieces of text matter.

Examples:

- Form hints
- Placeholders
- Tooltips
- Confirmation messages

Microcopy should always reduce uncertainty.

---

# Empty States

Every empty state should:

- Explain why it's empty.
- Tell the user what to do next.

Example:

No reviews yet.

Be the first to share your experience.

---

# Error Messages

Never blame the user.

Good:

Something went wrong.
Please try again.

Bad:

Invalid request.

---

# Success Messages

Keep them short.

Example:

Business profile updated successfully.

---

# Business Descriptions

Descriptions should be:

- Informative
- Honest
- Easy to read

Avoid exaggerated marketing language.

---

# Business Language

Describe businesses honestly.

Focus on facts before opinions.

Highlight what customers need to know.

Avoid exaggerated claims unless they can be verified.

---

# SEO Writing

Write for humans first.

Search engines second.

Never stuff keywords.

---

# Key Principle

Every word should earn its place.

If a sentence does not help the user, remove it.

If a sentence can be made shorter without losing meaning, make it shorter.

If a word can be removed without changing the meaning, remove it.

Clarity is always more important than clever writing.
